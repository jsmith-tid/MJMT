Train in Thailand

Your Complete Guide to Training at MJ Five Points Muay Thai

Why Train in Thailand?

Training Muay Thai in its birthplace is an experience unlike any other. Thailand is the authentic home of Muay Thai, where the sport is deeply rooted in culture and tradition. At MJ Five Points Muay Thai in Phuket, you'll train with experienced coaches in a genuine gym environment—not a tourist resort, but a real place where serious training happens every day.

This is your opportunity to immerse yourself in the art, improve your technique dramatically, and experience the discipline and respect that define Muay Thai.

📍 Location & Getting Here

Where We Are

MJ Five Points Muay Thai is located in Phuket, Thailand. The gym is centrally accessible and situated in a real, authentic setting. We're not in a resort area—this is where locals train.

Arriving in Phuket

Phuket International Airport (HKT) is the main gateway. Most international flights arrive here. The airport is well-connected and transfer to the gym is straightforward. We'll provide detailed directions and can recommend pickup services.

Ground Transport

Taxis, Grab (Southeast Asia's ride-sharing app), and private transport are all readily available. We'll provide specific directions and can arrange pickup if needed. The journey from the airport to the gym is typically 30-45 minutes depending on traffic.

🏠 Accommodation

Important Note: Accommodation is not included in training packages unless specifically stated. However, we understand that finding safe, suitable accommodation can feel overwhelming when planning from abroad.

We Help You Choose

Once you enquire about training, we'll send you a curated list of trusted accommodation options near the gym. We personally recommend places we've verified as safe, clean, and suitable for visiting trainers.

Budget Options

Phuket offers accommodation at various price points. Budget guesthouses, mid-range hotels, and more comfortable options are all available within walking distance or a short taxi ride from the gym.

Safety & Comfort

All recommended accommodation is in safe, well-established areas with reliable amenities. We only recommend places we're confident about. Your safety and comfort during training time is important to us.

🏥 Health, Safety & Insurance

Your Safety Matters

Professional coaching with safety as our priority. Our coaches are experienced in training people of all levels and prioritize proper form and injury prevention. We'll assess your experience level and tailor training to your needs.

Travel Insurance

Highly recommended. Travel insurance covering sports/training activities is essential. Many standard policies don't cover training injuries, so verify coverage includes Muay Thai training before purchasing.

Medical Support

Thailand has excellent medical facilities. Phuket has several high-quality hospitals with English-speaking staff. In the unlikely event of injury, professional medical support is readily available.

Before You Come

If you have any pre-existing conditions or injuries, please inform us during your inquiry. We can discuss how to accommodate your needs and ensure training is suitable for your health profile.

📋 Visa & Entry Requirements

Thailand's entry requirements vary by nationality. Here's what you need to know:

Visa Exemption

Many nationalities qualify for visa-free entry for 30 days (or 60 days for certain countries). Check Thailand's official immigration website for your nationality's specific allowances.

Tourist Visa

If your stay extends beyond the visa-free period, tourist visas are available. These are typically 60 days and can often be extended. Your hotel can assist with extension paperwork.

Passport Requirements

Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months from your intended date of entry. Make sure your passport is in good condition—damaged or questionable passports can cause entry issues.

🥊 Training Environment

MJ Five Points Muay Thai is an authentic training gym, not a resort or tourist facility. Here's what to expect:

Real Gym Environment

Class Structure

🌴 Living in Phuket

Phuket is a vibrant location with plenty to offer outside training hours:

Food & Dining

Thai cuisine is incredible and affordable. From street food to restaurants, you'll eat amazingly well on any budget. Healthy, fresh food is everywhere—important for fueling your training.

Cost of Living

Phuket is very affordable compared to most Western countries. Accommodation, food, and daily expenses are reasonable, meaning your training investment goes further.

Recovery Activities

Massage, stretching, and recovery facilities are abundant and inexpensive. Many students book traditional Thai massage during their stay—excellent for recovery and relaxation after intense training.

Free Time

Between training sessions, explore the local area. Walk to nearby restaurants, relax, or engage with the local community. Balance training intensity with rest and cultural immersion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the gym clean and hygienic?

Yes. Despite being authentic and not a resort, we maintain professional hygiene standards. Equipment is regularly cleaned and maintained.

Can I arrive early or stay longer?

Absolutely. We can accommodate extended stays and modified schedules. Contact us to discuss your specific needs.

What if I get injured?

We prioritize injury prevention, but if injury occurs, professional medical support is available. This is why travel insurance is essential. We'll help coordinate medical care if needed.

Can I train if I'm out of shape?

Yes, absolutely. Our coaches work with all fitness levels. We'll assess your condition and design a program appropriate for you. Come as you are.

Do I need to know Thai?

No. Our coaches speak English. While learning basic Thai phrases is fun and rewarding, it's not necessary for training.

What's the weather like in Phuket?

Tropical climate year-round. Warm and humid. There's a monsoon season, but training continues regardless. Pack light, breathable clothing.
