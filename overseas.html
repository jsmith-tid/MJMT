Before you travel

Plan a visit to Phuket.

Start by discussing your training goals and dates. Travel support and training arrangements depend on what can be agreed for your visit.

Location & getting here

Training is in Phuket, Thailand. Phuket International Airport is the island’s main airport. Enquire to discuss the gym’s current address, directions and your arrival plans before arranging transport.

If you need transfer advice or pickup assistance, include that in your enquiry. Any assistance is subject to discussion; no transfer is included or confirmed here.

Accommodation

Arrange accommodation separately after discussing your training location and dates. Ask whether nearby recommendations can be provided and confirm costs and bookings directly with your chosen accommodation.

Accommodation recommendations or assistance depend on the arrangements discussed for your visit.

What to bring

Plan for lightweight training clothes, water and a towel. Ask what equipment you should bring and what may be available at the gym before packing.

Health & safety

Consider travel insurance that covers your intended training activities. Raise any participation or safety questions before training, and discuss medical concerns privately with a qualified professional.

Ask about local medical facilities and any assistance that may be available. Medical support or coordination by the gym is subject to discussion.

Visa & entry

Check current entry requirements with the relevant Thai embassy or consulate for your nationality and intended stay before booking travel.

Before you arrive

  • Discuss training dates, focus and Master Jud’s involvement.
  • Agree session arrangements, availability and any costs.
  • Confirm the gym address and arrange transport and accommodation.
  • Ask what equipment to bring and what help may be available.

Training days and times are enquiry dependent. Any session times discussed refer to Phuket time (ICT, UTC+7).
