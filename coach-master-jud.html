Master Jud

Boxing Name: Banluesak Chuenyim

Surachai Chanmanee, Founder & Head Coach

About

Master Jud is the founder and head coach of Master Jud Muay Thai Coaching. With decades of experience in authentic Muay Thai, he has worked with world-class fighters and brings unparalleled expertise to every training session.

Training Style

Inspired by his father, who was also a boxer, at age 7, Surachai began his Muay Thai journey in rural areas. After numerous matches, he moved to Bangkok where he fought under the name of Banluesak Chuenyim.

After retiring, his Muay Thai journey continued where he trained many at the Kiattivanaram camp. In those years, he trained renowned fights like Chamuakthong, Thepyut, and Yodthongchai Kiattivanaram. He later trained several world champions at the Por Pramuk Gym and helped to win the Best Muay Thai Camp in Thailand award in 2001.

Since then, he has trained numerous world champions such as Buakaw Por Pramuk (now Buakaw Banchamek), Namsaknoi Yudthakarnkamtorn (the King of Muay Thai Dance), Chokdee, Sakaeo, Phajonsuek, Pornpitak, Ratchamongkol, Thepyut, and Chamuakthong as well as Prasawan Por Pramuk, who became a world champion in both Muay Thai and boxing (WBA), and Kompayak (WBC) from Por Pramuk Gym.

International Experience

Master Jud has traveled extensively to teach and conduct seminars abroad, particularly in the United States at Fivepoints Academy in New York, Chaiyo MuayThai, and Muaythaiiyarin, among other gyms. He has helped many fighters achieve championship titles. He travels to the United States annually to teach and conduct seminars.

Best For

Intermediate to advanced practitioners, serious fighters, and anyone looking for professional-level coaching from a world-class trainer.
