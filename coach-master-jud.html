About

Master Jud is the founder and head coach of Master Jud Muay Thai Coaching. With decades of experience in authentic Muay Thai, he has worked with world-class fighters and brings unparalleled expertise to every training session.

Specialties

  • Advanced technique development
  • Fighter conditioning and strategy
  • Professional-level coaching
  • Mentorship and career guidance

Training Style

Master Jud's approach is rooted in authentic, old-school Muay Thai principles. He focuses on real, functional techniques that work in and outside the ring. No fluff, no shortcuts—just pure, effective training.

Best For

Intermediate to advanced practitioners, serious fighters, and anyone looking for professional-level coaching from a world-class trainer.
