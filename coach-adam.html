Coach Adam

Boxing Name: Haodong Kiatploypailin

Senior Trainer

About

Coach Adam is a senior trainer specializing in technique refinement and fighter development. With years of experience, he helps fighters develop their unique fighting style and maximize their potential.

Specialties

  • Technique refinement
  • Fighting style development
  • Intermediate to advanced training
  • Personalized coaching

Training Style

Coach Adam (Mr. Korin Khaengyuenyon) started boxing at the age of 15. Affiliated with the Kiatploypailin boxing camp, he is a stadium veteran holding well over 100 fights and began training at the age of 27.

He serves as an assistant to Master Jud, has been a sparring partner for Buakaw and other famous boxers in the P. Pramuk camp. Previously he has been a trainer at Banchamek Muay Thai gym, Bangkok, Thailand and Rachanon Boxing Camp, Bangkok, Thailand, Pickpack gym and many other well-known Muay Thai camps both domestically and internationally.

Best For

Intermediate and advanced practitioners looking to refine their technique and develop a distinctive fighting style. Great for students who want personalized attention.
