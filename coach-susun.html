Coach Susun

Boxing Names: Yod Susan, S. Choknitiya, Phet Susan M. Yuddan, and The knee King of Nakhon Si Thammarat

Professional Coach

About

Kru Sun started boxing at age 7, and he holds a WBC Muay Thai Thailand 135 lb professional Champion Title.

Kru Sun started teaching boxing at age 30 at Lions MMA gym, Phuket Thailand. He holds a B-License and C-License for certified boxing instructor from the Sports Authority of Thailand.

He boasts a fighting record with 300+ fights and a winning record.

Specialties

  • Power development
  • Advanced combinations
  • Striking mechanics
  • Force generation techniques

Training Style

Coach Susun emphasizes proper mechanics and biomechanics to generate explosive power. His coaching style focuses on efficient movement and maximum force output.

Best For

Intermediate to advanced practitioners wanting to develop more powerful strikes and master advanced combination techniques.
