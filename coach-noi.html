Coach Noi

Boxing Name: Champathong Kiatongyuth

Professional Coach

About

Coach Noi started Muay Thai at 7 Years old at Camp Kiatongyuth, Bangkok Thailand and became a South of Thailand champion at just aged 12 yeards old in the 40kg category.

His Muay Thai record boasts over 300 fights in his career. He started coaching at just 22 years old while still being an active fighter. He has been a trainer at Pinyo muay Thai, Koh Samui and has done international training in China and Japan.

Specialties

  • Fundamental technique
  • Beginner-friendly coaching
  • Foundation building
  • Student confidence development

Training Style

Coach Noi's approach is encouraging and supportive. He breaks down techniques into simple, understandable steps and ensures each student builds a solid foundation before advancing.

Best For

Beginners and those new to Muay Thai. Perfect for students who want clear, step-by-step instruction and a supportive learning environment.
