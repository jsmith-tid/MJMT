About

Nawaphon brings professional fighting experience and a wealth of knowledge to every training session. His background as a professional trainer gives him unique insights into what actually works in competition.

Specialties

  • Professional fighting experience
  • Competitive mentorship
  • Fighter development

Training Style

With over 16 years of experience in training Muay Thai fighters, Nawaphon Chanmanee has worked with an incredible range of fighters, from being an instructor C-Class to training the Ukrainian national team and sparring with Buakaw. His approach is focused on real-world application, ensuring that every technique and strategy is effective in the ring. He emphasizes practical training that prepares fighters for actual competition, drawing from his extensive experience in the sport.

Best For

Anyone interested in professional fighting, competitors seeking mentorship, and advanced practitioners wanting to learn from someone with authentic professional experience.
