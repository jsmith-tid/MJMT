Coach Nin

Boxing Name: Nilwiset Kaennorsing

Professional Coach

About

Coach Nin (Mr. Therdkosol Wonglakorn) is an expert in conditioning and ring strategy. With professional experience, he knows what it takes to compete at the highest level and passes that knowledge to his students.

Specialties

  • Advanced conditioning
  • Ring strategy and tactics
  • Competitive fight preparation
  • Endurance and strength training

Training Style

Kru Nin Holds the North Eastern Region 100 lb Championship, the Grand Fish Kitchenware Tournament 105 lb Championship, was the Poom Suea Tournament runner-up, and has accrued many other titles in his career.

Best For

Kru Nin has worked as a trainer for numerous gyms including Phuket Fight Club, and Lions MMA in Phuket, Thailand, and has had well over 300 fights in his career.
