## Review

The existing charcoal and gold styling is retained through style.css with prototype.css refinements. English is the only language offered.
Old coach pages, coaches.html and th/ files remain as unlinked historical materials with noindex. They are not supported visitor routes. Retired prices remain only in those materials, not in the English visitor journey.
No payments, simulated availability, reference numbers or confirmation behavior run. booking.js is an inert compatibility file for archived pages.
Formspree ID myzerkny is retained only as an unverified placeholder. Submission is intercepted locally; the demo button is disabled without JavaScript. The form never sends or saves data. WhatsApp placeholder links are retained and open a demo notice.
Home uses photo 13 as a square hero, photo 02 for history, and 07/09 as archive photographs. No dates or identities are inferred in captions.
Existing user-added external fighter links are retained on the MJ profile. Links do not verify coaching relationships.
