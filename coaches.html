Meet Our Coaching Team

World-class trainers dedicated to your Muay Thai journey

Master Jud

Founder & Head Coach

Decades of experience in authentic Muay Thai and founder of this coaching academy. With world-class fighters trained under his guidance, he brings unparalleled expertise to advanced technique development and fighter conditioning. Inspired by his father, Master Jud has trained numerous world champions including Buakaw Por Pramuk and traveled extensively to teach seminars internationally.

Coach Nawaphon

Professional Trainer

With over 16 years of experience training Muay Thai fighters, Nawaphon brings professional fighting experience and a focus on real-world application. He has worked with the Ukrainian national team, sparred with Buakaw, and emphasizes practical training that prepares fighters for actual competition, drawing from his extensive professional background.

Coach Adam

Senior Trainer

Senior trainer specializing in technique refinement and fighter development. With a stadium record of over 100 fights and experience at renowned camps including Banchamek Muay Thai gym in Bangkok, Coach Adam serves as an assistant to Master Jud and has been a sparring partner for Buakaw and other famous boxers. Perfect for intermediate to advanced practitioners refining their style.

Coach Susun

Professional Coach - Power Specialist

Known as "The Knee King," Coach Susun started boxing at age 7 and holds a WBC Muay Thai Thailand 135 lb professional Champion Title. With over 300 fights and certified instructor licenses from Thailand's Sports Authority, he emphasizes proper biomechanics to generate explosive power and efficient movement for maximum force output in striking.

Coach Noi

Professional Coach - Beginner Specialist

Started Muay Thai at age 7 and became South of Thailand champion at 12. With over 300 fights and international training experience in China and Japan, Coach Noi takes an encouraging and supportive approach. He breaks down techniques into simple steps, perfect for beginners building a solid foundation with clear instruction and a welcoming environment.

Coach Nin

Professional Coach - Ring Strategy Specialist

Expert in conditioning and ring strategy with extensive professional experience. Coach Nin holds the North Eastern Region 100 lb Championship and Grand Fish Kitchenware Tournament 105 lb Championship. With over 300 fights and training experience at prominent Phuket gyms including Phuket Fight Club and Lions MMA, he specializes in competitive fight preparation.
